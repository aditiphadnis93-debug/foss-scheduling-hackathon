## Cause list as a calendar
This is a UI/UX idea.

A cause list (or causelist) in the Indian judicial system is the official daily schedule or calendar published by a court, listing all the cases scheduled to be heard by a specific judge or bench on a particular working day.

Every role player will have their own view of the calendar. Conisder it like the meetings on an Outlook calendar. The meeting invites/cause list will contain details of the cases, the court room information, the purpose of the hearing.


### Judges view
- A calendar view like an outlook calendar. The judge can view the cases for the day/week/month.
